The Most Dense City Ever Built

Six distinct districts. Thousands of NPCs with lives, routines, and dialogue. Neon-drenched streets, corporate skyscrapers, and back-alley gangs coexist in a city that never sleeps and never forgives.

// V & Johnny

Two Souls. One Body.

A relic in V's head carries the engram of Johnny Silverhand — a dead rockstar anarchist played by Keanu Reeves. Your relationship with Johnny is the heart of Cyberpunk 2077. It is extraordinary.

The spy-thriller expansion adds Dogtown — a lawless district controlled by a paramilitary faction. New quest lines, new endings, new cyberware, and Idris Elba as a NUSA agent. Essential.
