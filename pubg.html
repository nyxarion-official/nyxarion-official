// Game Overview

What is PUBG Mobile?

Developed by KRAFTON and published by Level Infinite, PUBG Mobile drops 100 players onto a shrinking island. Every match is a fight for survival — loot, strategize, and outlast everyone. The last squad standing wins. Simple concept. Infinite depth.

🪂
Battle Royale Core
100 players parachute onto a massive map. Resources are scarce. The safe zone shrinks. Every decision can be your last.
Core Mode
🔫
Realistic Gunplay
Bullet physics, realistic recoil patterns, and attachment systems mean gun mastery separates the casual from the elite.
Skill-Based
👥

Solo, Duo, or Squad of four. Teamwork, callouts, and coordination define championship-level play.

// Pakistan Esports · Spotlight

Team 4Thrives Esports

🎮

4THRIVES ESPORTS

Pakistan's Pride on the Global Stage

🇵🇰 Pakistan · South Asia Region · Est. December 1, 2024
South Asia S-Tier Competitor PMGO 2026 S1 No Sponsor · Pure Talent

Born on December 1, 2024 — not in a corporate gaming house, but through WhatsApp calls, shared dreams, and relentless grind. Manager Usman "Shaheen" Tariq handpicked players for synergy over fame. In less than nine months they became the first Pakistani team ever to reach the Grand Finals of an S-Tier PUBG Mobile event, finishing 7th at the PMWC 2025 with 1.3M+ peak viewers watching.

// Journey So Far

4Thrives Timeline

December 1, 2024
4Thrives is Born
Manager Shaheen forms the team via WhatsApp. Original lineup: FALAK, IQ, Nocki, T24OP, CAIRO. No sponsor. Just hunger.
🇵🇰 Pakistan's Next Chapter Begins
Early 2025
PMSL CSA Spring 2025 — 3rd Place
Their first major breakthrough. Finished 3rd at PMSL Central & South Asia Spring, earning $18,750 and a direct qualification slot for the Esports World Cup 2025.
$18,750 Earned · EWC Slot Secured
July–August 2025
Esports World Cup 2025 — Riyadh
First Pakistani team to qualify for EWC. Competed at the most prestigious esports event in history with a $70M+ total prize pool.
🏅 Historic First for Pakistan Esports
August 1–3, 2025
PMWC Grand Finals — 7th Place
Survival Stage performance was dominant: 2nd overall, 111 kills, 65 eliminations, 2 Chicken Dinners. Grand Finals finish: 7th with 109 points. $157,000 total earned. FALAK ranked 6th fragger and 3rd highest damage. 1.3M+ peak viewers.
🥇 First PK Team at S-Tier Grand Finals · $157K
September 6, 2025
Roster Acquired by Inner Circle Esports
All five players and Shaheen move to Inner Circle Esports under a new organisation deal. 4Thrives the brand continues.
December 2025
Rebuild — New Roster Forms
Inner Circle Esports releases the roster. 4Thrives reacquires IQ, Nocki, FALAK, T24OP and CAIRO. Shaheen returns. CAIRO then departs to Guts & Glory. HUZAIFA (former AS i8 Esports IGL) joins December 22.
May 14–17, 2026
PMGO S1 — South Asia Finals, Lahore 🏆
4Thrives were directly invited as defending South Asian titans. Finished 1st at the SA Finals in Lahore, Pakistan. Won $9,000 and 60 PMGC Points. Qualified as South Asia's top seed for the Main Event.
🥇 SA Champions · $9,000 · Jakarta Bound
June 2–7, 2026
PMGO 2026 Season 1 — Jakarta 🔥
Currently competing at the Tennis Indoor Stadium Senayan, Jakarta, Indonesia. 32 of the world's best teams. $500,000 on the line. Pakistan's eyes are watching.
